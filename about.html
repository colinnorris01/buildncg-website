About Us

Family-owned, locally operated, and committed to transforming homes across Charlotte, NC — with full interior design, 3D rendering, and construction services under one roof.

Our Story

Building Dreams in the Queen City

At Norris Construction Group, we take pride in delivering high-quality construction and remodeling services to clients in Charlotte, NC and the surrounding areas. As a family-owned and locally operated company, we understand the importance of trust, transparency, and exceptional craftsmanship.

Our experienced team works collaboratively to bring your vision to life — from initial interior design concepts and photorealistic 3D renderings through construction and final finishes. From kitchen and bathroom renovations to new build construction, we treat every project as if it were our own home.

With a passion for what we do and a commitment to excellence, we specialize in creating breathtaking spaces that reflect the unique style and vision of each homeowner. We partner with Houzz Pro to give every client a dedicated project portal — featuring 3D renderings, real-time scheduling, estimates and invoices, mood boards, and material selections all in one place. Real products from fixtures to furniture are placed directly into your rendering so you see exactly what your finished space will look like before we ever pick up a hammer. Let us help transform your space today.
